# Beanery — brand & UI direction

**Positioning:** premium casual dining expressed through contemporary European café culture.
Above an ordinary café, below fine dining. Premium without being formal.

Hierarchy on every page: **brand → experience → story → coffee + food → menu → transaction.**
The menu never leads.

## Brand pillars (recurring device, numbered 01–05)
01 Coffee · 02 Kitchen · 03 Culture · 04 Hospitality · 05 Craft.
They appear on the homepage as a five-cell ruled grid and set the language for inner pages.

## Voice
Warm, conversational, first-person plural. Specific over promotional ("dialled twice a day",
"when the bread runs out, the sandwich board closes"). No adjectives we can't prove.
Hero line: *European café culture, thoughtfully served.*

## Art direction
Approved Beanery palette only — flat fills, no gradients, no blur/glass effects.
- **Latte `#B78765`** — key shade; the closing reservation band, warm surfaces, accents on dark.
- **Dark Espresso `#5E2B17`** — all ink, navigation, buttons, and the dark sections (signature collection, testimonials, footer).
- **Warm Camel `#AF6E43`** — numerals, index marks, tonal variation.
- **Terracotta `#A35730`** — editorial labels, italic emphasis, the seasonal poster banner.
- Grounds: natural white `#FBF8F4`, cream `#EFE3D8`, latte beige `#DFCBB9`. Body copy `#6E4A34`, metadata `#96755C`.
- Warm family holds 85–90% of every page; dark espresso sections stay under ~20%.
- **Green, 10–15% max and functional only:** Forest `#2E5D36` for ingredient, produce, vegetarian and freshness callouts; Sage `#6B8F5A` / Matcha `#A7B88F` as subtle supporting accents. Never a background or general brand colour.
- Zero corner radius throughout (carried from Modernist). Structure comes from 1px rules, not cards or shadows.

## Typography
- **Playfair Display** — hero statements, section titles, dish and origin names, numerals.
- **Cormorant Garamond** italic — pull-quotes, chef/barista notes, one-line brand asides only.
- **Archivo** — nav, buttons, labels, body, metadata. Labels: 10–10.5px, `.2em` tracking, uppercase.

## Layout system
Full-bleed cinematic hero with type overlapping imagery · split-screen storytelling · ruled
five-cell pillar grid · asymmetric behind-the-bar mosaic · horizontal rails (signature, brews,
experiences) · magazine three-up for journal · day-part selector for the experience section.
Section rhythm alternates: full-bleed → ruled editorial → split → dark poster.

## Photography
Warm, cinematic, never stock-commercial. Four registers:
**Food** — restaurant-level plating, sauce work, chef's hands finishing, table presentation.
**Coffee** — extraction, grind, pour, latte art, equipment, origin.
**People** — candid, unposed, mid-service or mid-conversation.
**Space & detail** — west light, marble, linen, crockery, crumb, beans.
Every image is an `<image-slot>` placeholder captioned with the shot to brief.

## Motion
Slow and intentional: 0.78–1.1s reveals on a single easing curve, 1.4–1.6s image scale on hover,
no bounce, no parallax gimmicks, no constant motion. Motion intensity is a tweak (restrained / soft / rich).

## Responsive
Desktop-first, with a mobile layer that keeps oversized type and full-width photography:
multi-column grids collapse to one, layered heroes become photo + type stack, rails become
82vw swipe cards, and the nav becomes an editorial full-screen menu.

## Brand test applied to each section
"Could this belong to any other café?" If yes, it gets a Beanery-specific device: a numbered
pillar, a named origin, a chef's note, a day-part, or a scored flavour profile.
